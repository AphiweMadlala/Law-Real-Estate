About This Property

This masterpiece on Heron Crescent invites you to indulge in luxury living at its finest. This immaculate residence offers a wealth of features to enhance your lifestyle.

Upon arrival, be greeted by a grand glass double-volume entrance hall adorned with a dazzling chandelier, setting the tone for the elegance that awaits. Entertain guests effortlessly in the open-plan dining area, seamlessly connected to the lounge boasting a cozy wood fireplace and a TV room with air conditioning for utmost comfort.

The kitchen is a culinary haven, equipped with quartz stone countertops, a gas hob, built-in oven, microwave, and a convenient hot and cold tap with a filtered water system. Sliding windows offer seamless service to the patio, where you'll find a prep bowl and a braai area overlooking the lush garden and inviting pool.

The bedrooms exude comfort and style, with the second bedroom featuring high ceilings, blinds, and a dressing area, while the third bedroom offers a study area and an en-suite bathroom. The luxurious main bedroom boasts an air conditioner, folding doors onto a balcony with pool views, a dressing room, and a lavish en-suite bathroom with his and her basins, a shower, and a freestanding bath, all complemented by stunning views.

For added convenience, this home features three garages, including one single and one double with storage space, as well as staff quarters with an en-suite bathroom. Upstairs, discover a haven of relaxation and productivity with an aluminum glass encased study boasting views of the
4th hole, and a gym with folding doors opening onto a balcony overlooking the pool.

Additional features include linen cupboards, a skylight between the study bedrooms and gym, geysers with heat pumps, a sprinkler system throughout the garden, a DStv dish, prepaid electricity, and an inverter 5.1kva with a lithium battery.

Don't miss the opportunity to call this exquisite property home. Schedule a viewing today and step into a world of luxury and sophistication.
