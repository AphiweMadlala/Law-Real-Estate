About This Property

Step into this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom home, set on a generous 1 487 m² stand in Bramley which is widely regarded as one of the most desirable pockets of Bramley, bordering Waverley and Savoy in both feel and positioning. Situated within the Waverley–Savoy gated community, this home offers space, character, and a rare sense of tranquility and security.

The home welcomes you with a grand entrance hall, pressed ceilings, and parquet flooring throughout much of the house. A spacious lounge and family dining room flow into a well-kept kitchen, while three full bathrooms ensure comfort and practicality for family living.

One of the standout features of this property is its location. Within walking distance to the Waverley Synagogue, making it especially appealing to families seeking both lifestyle and community convenience.

The property enjoys both front and back gardens, with a beautifully maintained hedge surrounding the front of the home, acting as a natural privacy screen. The swimming pool, positioned at the rear of the property, creates an ideal setting for family gatherings and relaxed summer entertaining.

Additional features include two garages, ample driveway parking, a domestic room, and expansive outdoor space offering flexibility for entertaining, play, or future enhancements.

Positioned between two closed-off roads, this home enjoys a heightened level of privacy and security, further supported by CAP patrols in the area—making it a true lock-up-and-go option in a family-friendly environment.
