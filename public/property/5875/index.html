About This Property

Positioned within the prestigious confines of Devonshire Avenue in Bryanston, lies a haven of modern yet classic contemporary style.

Welcome to an exquisite residence where no expense has been spared in crafting the epitome of luxurious living.

Entertain guests with ease in the expansive reception areas, featuring an open-plan lounge and dining area, complemented by a casual reading room and an entertainment room complete with a pool table. Intimate spaces complimented with fireplaces offer the perfect setting for cozy evenings spent with loved ones. All the living spaces are fitted with the finest engineered wooden flooring ensuring the quality is a lifetime of use.

Be captivated by the finest finishes on every corner of this remarkable home. The kitchen, a masterpiece of design, boasts a blu-line design and finish with top-of-the-line Miele appliances, catering to the most discerning culinary connoisseur.

Upstairs, three bedrooms await, two of which boast lavish dressing rooms. The master suite is a space that has been well thought out and perfectly designed to accommodate a tranquil and relaxing space with all the extras in the en suite bathroom and walk in dressers. A pajama lounge provides a retreat for relaxation.

Additional highlights include a guest bedroom downstairs, staff accommodation, and a manicured garden with irrigation and a heated pool with an automatic pool cover adorned by imported tiles.

Outside, a separate, covered entertainment area with a built-in gas braai and kitchenette beckons for unforgettable gatherings under the stars.

The property also boasts air-conditioning, solar heating, and fireplaces for year-round comfort.
With three garages and parking for six cars, this home effortlessly combines style with practicality.
Situated within a private security estate with pets allowed, and featuring two geysers and aluminum windows, this residence offers the ultimate in modern luxury estate living.

Extras abound, including a 15-kilowatt LPG fuel generator system, a solar inverter with 12-kilowatt lithium batteries, electronic pool cover, patio blinds, solid wooden floors, American shutters, and double glazing on all windows.

Don't miss the opportunity to make this your own, where every detail has been meticulously curated to offer a lifestyle of unparalleled sophistication and comfort.

Schedule your viewing today and step into the epitome of luxury living.
