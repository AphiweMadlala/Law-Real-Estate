About This Property

Welcome to your new dream home, a stunning multilevel property that is sure to take your breath away! This immaculate property is designed to provide you with luxury living like you've never experienced before. Situated in a prime location, this brand new ultra-modern Japanese-inspired theme property offers spectacular panoramic views and is surrounded by an exquisitely zero maintenance landscaped garden with a calming waterfall feature that welcomes you the moment you step inside.

The kitchen is open plan with an island on pure opulence and provides ample space for gatherings and entertaining friends. The formal lounge with a fireplace is perfect for creating a cozy ambiance during the colder winter months, while the open plan lounge is ideal for enjoying quality time with its built in bar and wine cellar.

Upstairs: All 4 bedrooms are spacious and come with en-suite bathrooms, providing the ultimate in privacy and convenience. The luxurious primary suite features custom glass doors, a private deck with views of Hyde park, spa-like bathroom, and a large walk-in closet.

The property is fitted with aluminum window frames and skylights, creating a modern and stylish feel that is second to none. The swimming pool is a wonderful addition that adds to the ambiance and provides the perfect place to relax and unwind during the warmer summer months. With staff accommodation included, you can rest easy knowing that your domestic staff will have a comfortable place to stay.

This magnificent property is truly an oasis of luxury and comfort that you can call home. Don't miss out on the opportunity to own this piece of paradise and start living the life you've always dreamed of.
