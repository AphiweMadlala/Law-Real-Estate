About This Property

A home where absolutely no expense has been spared. This Dream Home is set in an exclusive secure estate in a prime position in Morningside

We are proud and honoured to present this sensational, one-of-a-kind residence.

This modern masterpiece is a testament to luxury living, designed to seamlessly blend with nature, whilst offering the utmost in comfort and privacy. Consisting of six bedrooms, six and a half bathrooms, and a wealth of premium features, this truly is the epitome of contemporary elegance.

Upon stepping through the grand entrance, you'll immediately be captivated by the abundant natural light that floods every corner of this home, thanks to its strategically designed floor-to-ceiling windows. The interior spaces are bathed in sunlight throughout the day, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere.

Inside, you'll discover an array of exceptional features, a stylish lounge, formal dining room, bar area and a modern family TV room. The spacious chefs kitchen is fitted with top tier appliances and a walk in fridge.

All the living spaces stack open onto a generous entertainers patio which leads onto the spacious and meticulously landscaped garden and sparkling blue pool.

With garaging for four cars, you'll have ample space for your vehicle collection or guest parking.

The property includes separate staff quarters, adding an extra layer of functionality. The estate offers a guard house with 24hour security.

This is a rare opportunity to own a truly exceptional property that embodies the very best in modern luxury living. Don't miss your chance – this remarkable home won't last long.
