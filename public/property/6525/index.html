About This Property

In the prestigious heart of Ferndale, Randburg, lies a property unlike any other, a seamless fusion of sophisticated residential comfort and dynamic commercial utility. Designed with discerning professionals in mind, this expansive property offers a rare opportunity to live and work in unparalleled style, where every detail caters to both refined living and seamless business operations.

The residence exudes modern elegance, with three generously proportioned bedrooms bathed in natural light, including a sumptuous main suite with private balcony access, your own personal retreat at the end of a productive day. Two stylish bathrooms, including a main en-suite, complement the flow of the home, while the living areas invite connection and relaxation. A sunlit lounge, complete with a home theatre and gas fireplace all of which opens effortlessly onto a dining space and outdoor patio, perfect for entertaining or unwinding. The gourmet kitchen is a culinary haven, fitted with sleek granite countertops, a double-door refrigerator alcove, twin induction cooktops, and space for dual wet appliances. A dedicated study or fourth bedroom with patio access provides a tranquil escape for moments of focus or creativity.

The adjoining commercial wing transforms the property into a powerhouse of productivity. A dedicated office suite includes four private offices and an impressive boardroom, all opening to a landscaped patio that blurs the lines between work and nature. A kitchenette and dual staff bathrooms enhance convenience. The ground floor offers an additional reception lounge, five versatile office spaces, a large studio area, and secure lockable storage accommodating everything from boutique enterprises to established businesses seeking prestige and convenience.

This property goes beyond expectations with its extensive amenities. Energy independence is ensured through 28 solar panels paired with two robust inverters and a backup generator ensuring constant connectivity and empower uninterrupted operations. Ample parking for 16 vehicles comprising eight covered carports and eight off-street bays welcomes both residents and clients with ease. Outdoors, a sparkling pool and manicured garden create a private oasis for relaxation or sophisticated gatherings, complemented by air conditioning, separate security systems for the home and office, and an electric fence for ultimate peace of mind.

Perfectly positioned close to the Randburg Magistrates Court, leading schools, bustling business hubs, and premier shopping destinations, this dual-purpose gem embodies accessibility, exclusivity, and investment potential in equal measure.

Opportunities like this are exceptionally rare. Whether you envision a prestigious business headquarters with luxurious on-site living, or a unique investment combining commercial and residential assets, this property redefines the standard for versatility and elegance.

Schedule your private viewing today and discover the extraordinary blend of lifestyle and productivity that only this Ferndale masterpiece can offer.
