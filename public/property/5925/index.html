About This Property

This Ultimate abode epitomises uniqueness and incomparable features, showcasing a blend of luxury, leisure, and spiritual elements that make it truly one-of-a-kind.

Within the secure confines of an exclusive Sandton estate lies this exceptional residence, architect-designed and finished to the highest international standards with impeccable attention to every detail.

From the palatial, hand-crafted front gates and 8m Eroka wood entranceway to the ten-pin bowling alley and extravagant entertainment area, this super-home offers one breathtaking feature after another, and is a matchless fusion of opulence, elegance and efficiency to accommodate luxurious modern family living in a whole-life environment.

The triple volume entrance hall features a free-floating double staircase with hand crafted balustrades and Swarovski Crystal chandelier as an introduction to spectacular open-plan reception areas bathed in the light and warmth that pour through floor-to-ceiling glass stacking doors.

These lead to a teak deck, a raised rim flow pool, a jacuzzi hot tub and a landscaped, manicured garden with dancing, colour-changing fountains.

An enclosed outdoor patio is a perfect casual entertainment space, complete with pizza oven and both gas and electric braai facilities.

Indoors, Crema Marfil marble slabs and tiles form an intricate “stone carpet” of the highest quality, and anchor the home’s signature palette of earth tones. This is echoed in rich, exotic wood finishes ranging from high gloss Mecasso ebony pieces and lacquered walnut to cross-grained mahogany and beautiful burl. To add depth and visual interest, many walls are wrapped, panelled or textured, while ceilings are lavishly layered with staggered geometric shapes illuminated by recessed lighting.

Glossy surfaces continue in the kitchen where Mecasso ebony cabinetry is paired with hand-carved duco panels and glass doors. Caesarstone splashbacks and countertops join seamlessly and create the backdrop for state-of-the-art Gaggenau appliances, integrated freezers, and a spacious walk-in fridge and pantry. The adjacent breakfast room and family room feature metallic finishes and more vibrant colours denoting a more relaxed family zone.

Egyptian, hand-carved cabinetry adds an artistic design to the study area and a contemporary workroom provides a secluded space to play, learn or work online

The guest-suite bedroom and five ensuite bedrooms each have a private living or lounging area leading to the balcony that wraps around the house and features Astroturf putting greens while providing expansive views of the gardens and beyond to Sandton. The perfect execution of interior detailing can clearly be seen in the main bedroom suite where the ceilings are staggered to create a floating illusion and stucco walls and fireplaces add sculptural form to the space. In the main bathroom, chestnut brown solid slabs of marble step up to a freestanding spa bath while an oversized shower leads to an outdoor shower. In the dressing room, an aesthetic combination of leather, aluminum and Burl is matched with the functionality of closets organized in grand style.

All of the bathrooms are different, but all boast floor to ceiling tiling and feature walls creatively panelled with statement deco pieces. The guest powder room also features a Versace mixer with a Swarovski crystal lever, marble slabs engraved with Versace patterns, and a platinum-coated basin mounted on a hand-carved and highly polished cabinet.

Leading to the basement level, a nine-panel Broadway Design artwork blends the past with the present, classic with contemporary, and is a fitting introduction to an entertainment and social area with billiard table and the wine tasting cellar with solid mahogany table and silver travertine floors, as well as a luxurious home theatre with popcorn bar, deeply comfortable cine-loungers and high quality audio-visual system.

The home also boasts a gymnasium with sauna and steam shower, a prayer room and private elevator and the features within the property are endless. Using the latest technology, the home is environmentally sustainable and is powered by an automated lighting and audio system with back-up battery operated solar panels.

With its unique architectural elements, exclusive furnishings and bright gracious layouts the home offers originality, exclusivity, beauty, style and quality beyond compare.
