About This Property

The home features four well-sized bedrooms and three full bathrooms. The open-plan kitchen, living and dining area creates a welcoming central space, ideal for both family living and entertaining. Located in the sought-after Pinehaven Country Estate, this property offers comfort, style and a secure lifestyle in a beautiful setting.
