About This Property

A Spacious Family Estate with Endless Possibilities

Welcome to a truly exceptional home and property that offers more than just a home—it offers an entire lifestyle. Situated on an exceptional piece of land that gives you the feeling of space and freedom, this estate is perfect for large families, entertainers, or anyone who loves having room to breathe.

The main house is warm, welcoming, and built with both comfort and functionality in mind. As you arrive, you’re greeted with ample garage and parking space....
Step inside and you’ll find two exquisite spacious living rooms that flow into a formal dining area, perfect for hosting family dinners or entertaining guests. The kitchen is roomy and well-equipped, ideal for anyone who loves to cook.

Downstairs also features a guest toilet, as well as a bedroom with its own en suite bathroom—ideal for visitors or extended family. Upstairs features the following.... there’s a cozy Pajama lounge, two gorgeous spacious bedrooms with a shared full bathroom. A third lovely spacious bedroom en suite with The stand out highlight main bedroom, offering its own private balcony and a beautifully finished en suite bathroom, giving you a peaceful retreat at the end of the day. And if that’s not enough for you, this home was built with all intentions and purposes as an entertainers’ delight.

With one dedicated entertainment room off the left of the house which leads out onto the incredible outdoor living area which truly shines through. There’s a sparkling pool for those hot summer days, a firepit-style area perfect for relaxed evenings under the stars, and a dedicated entertainment room with its own toilet—great for gatherings, movie nights, or just enjoying your own chilled out space.

The separate cottage is a fantastic bonus, complete with its own double garage, two bedrooms each with en suite bathrooms, and a fully functional kitchen. Whether you use it for guests, extended family, or as a rental opportunity, it adds incredible flexibility to the property.
