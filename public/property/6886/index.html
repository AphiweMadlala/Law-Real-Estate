About This Property

This property offers the ideal quintessential suburban lifestyle for the larger or growing family.

The floor plan features multiple living areas, a retro kitchen complete with gas stove offers ample storage and counter working space and leads onto the formal dining room. The spacious sunny lounge flows out to the wrap around patio for outdoor entertainment and overlooks the level garden.

The sleeping area includes 5 generously sized bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, master bedroom has a private en suite.
