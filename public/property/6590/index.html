About This Property

Grandly proportioned and flooded with natural light, it features a superb layout incorporating both formal and informal living and dining areas enhanced by soaring ceilings and quality fixtures and finishes.

Gourmet size kitchen and informal living space, opens out to the green gardens, a sanctuary of peace and privacy with alfresco living, complete with swimming pool plus jacuzzi.

Family excellence continues with the extensive accommodation comprising five generous bedrooms all en-suite.

All bedrooms are appointed with built-in wardrobes and the king size master features a walk-in wardrobe/dressing room and deluxe his and hers ensuite. Each bedroom benefits from a superb, sunny position offering views. 4 Bedrooms upstairs, 1 Bedroom downstairs.

Further highlights include a spacious study with direct garden access, which could be used as a children’s play room. The home also offers convenient, wonderful staff accommodation.

Features include exquisite oversized windows, an array of built in cupboard space.

Situated in a 24hr, fort-knox security estate with active patrol.
