About This Property

Discover contemporary family living in one of Meyersdal’s most sought-after secure residential enclosures. Designed with comfort, practicality and effortless entertaining in mind, this beautifully finished home offers generous living spaces, premium finishes and a low-maintenance lifestyle.

From the moment you step inside, you’ll appreciate the seamless flow of the open-plan design. The spacious lounge, dining area and ultra-modern kitchen blend together beautifully, creating the perfect setting for everyday family life and entertaining guests. The kitchen features sleek, contemporary finishes and serves as the true heart of the home.

The home’s open-plan living spaces flow effortlessly into an exceptional outdoor entertainment area, creating the perfect balance between indoor comfort and outdoor living. Overlooking the secure sparkling swimming pool, the covered patio features a built-in gas braai and ample space for entertaining family and friends throughout the year. The low-maintenance garden offers lush lawn areas and a beautiful jungle gym, making it an ideal space for children to play while adults relax and entertain.

A standout feature of the home’s design is the seamless connection between the interior and exterior. Bedrooms enjoy direct access to the entertainment area and garden, filling the home with natural light and creating a wonderful indoor-outdoor lifestyle that is both practical and inviting.

The dedicated entertainment lounge is complete with a stylish built-in bar and stacker doors that open completely onto the patio, allowing the entertainment spaces to become one expansive area for hosting guests.

Throughout the home, luxurious modern finishes and quality fittings create an elegant yet welcoming atmosphere. Contemporary wall paneling, premium fixtures and sophisticated design elements have been carefully selected to enhance the home’s refined, modern aesthetic, delivering a level of style that is both timeless and impressive.

Offering five generously sized bedrooms and three stylish bathrooms, including two luxurious en-suite bathrooms, this home provides ample space for growing families while maintaining privacy and comfort.

An installed inverter system provides peace of mind by keeping essential power running during load shedding, ensuring uninterrupted comfort throughout the year. Spacious garage with extra cupboards for storage, full outside bathroom.

Situated within a secure, access-controlled estate renowned for its excellent management, family-friendly atmosphere, parks and outstanding location, residents enjoy low levies without compromising on security or lifestyle. Conveniently located close to excellent schools, shopping centres, restaurants and major transport routes, this is a home that perfectly balances luxury and convenience.
