About This Property

Combining elements of paradise with a sense of harmony and sophisticated beauty, reflecting the magnificence of this architectural masterpiece.

Debatably the best home available for acquisition on the Johannesburg Real Estate landscape.

Situated in an extremely exclusive and highly sought after gated community. This is the only sensational site which overlooks the golf course all the way to the magnificent city backdrop.

Its status is nothing short of world class and right from the approach, the silhouette of its spanning cantelevered structures marks its sense of modern grandeur. From its concisely layed driveway paving to the final accents of its imported interior, nothing is out of place.

The home is comprised of 3 floor levels and while its primary orientation is north, the floor plates fan to catch vistas to the west and east, emmersing itself in the natural beauty it surrounds. Architecturally, the home boasts modern design principles, clean edges and only the finest finishings and fittings (all of which has been imported from Italy) all set between 4m high volumes!

The ground floor welcomes a reception area and four car garage which is visible from inside home and the levels above - perfect for displaying collectibles and super cars. The stairway in this home forms a strong connection both physically and visually - it is an artwork in itself.

The first floor truly exemplifies the exhibition like nature of this illustrious home with an open plan, modern living solution and four meter high volumes throughout its fanning floorplate. The space draws you into its interior marvels all the while painting a continuous vista of natural beauty, CCJ golf course and the rolling hills beyond. From a Layout perspective, the east-west wing of the floor plate is connected by the central circulation element - stairway and elevator shaft.

The east wing is comprised of a magnificent bar area and adjacent formal floating lounge enclosed by 4 m high glass, sliding doors and wrapped balcony. The west wing (all of which is north facing) is anchored by a dark-themed, and truly one of a kind, masterfully executed front of house kitchen and accompanying back of house kitchen space, from which the Italian marble-backed fireplace, server, and dining room area seeming float within the ambiance of the high volumed space. The pinnicle of the west wing is the highly bespoke living area, a livable exhibition of design the sprawls outward to the sunsets and has your sunderdowners covered.

The entire glazed facade comprised of oversize-4m sliding doors, emence and highly practical, serve both to connect and separate the interior to the expansive patio and pool areas by virtue of their versatility, allowing the space to be experienced as one continuous floor plate when open - perfect for entertainment. Another unsung practical feature to this home is its use of 2 separate guest bathrooms on the first floor which align with the entertainment aspect of the home.

The second floor is equally as impressive and host to four unique en-suite bedrooms - each with a full walk-in closet and elegantly finished bathrooms - and family lounge area. The main bedroom is nothing no short of sensational 6-star experience with only the finest in every aspect and department of the configuration.
