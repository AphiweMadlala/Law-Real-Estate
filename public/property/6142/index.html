About This Property

Desirably located within this security complex in the heart of Sonneveld, this newly built 3 bedroom 2 bathroom modern, single storey home is a great purchase.
The master bedroom is spacious, with built -in robe and an en-suite bathroom with shower. The 2nd and 3rd bedroom can accommodate double beds, have built-in robes and share a 2nd bathroom with bath-tub. All the finishes are modern and light; the house is tiled through-out. The living- dining room area is very spacious and there is an open plan kitchen to complete this beautiful family home. The house is capped off with double garaging and a spacious back yard. Additional features include a gas geyser and cooking hob, so power interruptions will not darken your day!
