About This Property

Set on a sprawling 2000 m² plot, this luxurious home boasts 600 m² under roof, with exceptional features throughout.

This residence exudes a sense of zen and tranquility, making it a peaceful retreat from the city while still being centrally located. The home includes 4 spacious bedrooms and 4 bathrooms, with the master bedroom located on the ground floor, offering direct access to the manicured garden and pool area. The master suite is a private oasis, featuring a Jacuzzi, walk-in closet, and a luxurious ensuite bathroom. The serene atmosphere is further enhanced by double-volume ceilings, skylights, and a tranquil koi pond atrium, adding to the peaceful ambiance.

The kitchen and multiple living areas flow seamlessly to the outdoor spaces, including a sparkling pool, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The property also offers staff accommodation, a double garage, and modern amenities such as an inverter for uninterrupted power.

One of the standout features of this property is the tennis court, which has the potential to be subdivided, offering the unique opportunity to own two pieces of land in one of the most prestigious roads in Atholl.

Ideally located, this home is within close proximity to some of Johannesburg’s most prestigious schools, including Crawford, St David’s, and St Mary’s. It’s also just minutes from premier shopping and entertainment destinations like Melrose Arch, Blu Bird, Sandton City, and Rosebank, with easy access to major highways.

This property offers not just a home, but an exceptional, tranquil lifestyle. Opportunities like this are rare—don’t miss your chance to own this one-of-a-kind gem in one of Johannesburg’s most sought-after neighborhoods!
