About This Property

Offers from R3 999 000 - Seller asking R4.2 Million

Tucked away in a secure and exclusive estate on Coleraine Drive, this beautifully styled 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom cluster strikes the perfect balance between luxury and easy, low-maintenance living. Designed with a sharp interior eye and finished to an exceptional standard, the home offers 227 m² of sophisticated living space on a 192 m² stand ideal for buyers looking for a lock-up-and-go lifestyle without compromising on space, privacy, or style.

From the moment you step inside, the clean lines, thoughtful textures, and quality finishes create a sense of calm and elegance. Light-filled open-plan living areas flow seamlessly, with engineered wood floors, designer lighting, and custom window finishes adding a warm, modern touch.

The Kitchen
The kitchen is sleek and functional, with Caesarstone countertops, a gas hob, Whirlpool oven, and space for a double-door fridge and two under-counter appliances. Smart storage ensures everything has its place, making it as practical as it is beautiful.

Living & Outdoor Space
The main lounge features bespoke cabinetry and ambient lighting, and opens through stack-back doors into a tranquil garden with an irrigated lawn and heated plunge pool—perfect for entertaining or just unwinding at the end of the day.
Upstairs, a cosy pyjama lounge with American-style shutters offers a quiet retreat, ideal for a second TV area, reading nook or study space.

Bedrooms
All three bedrooms are en-suite and thoughtfully designed with a mix of layered textures, excellent cupboard space, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow.
The main suite is a real standout, with a walk-in dressing area, spa-like bathroom (complete with a freestanding tub, double vanity, and luxurious finishes), and sliding doors leading onto a private balcony.
The second bedroom also includes a full en-suite and balcony access.
The third bedroom, ideal for guests or a home office, features a Juliet balcony with peaceful leafy views.

Extras You’ll Love

Guest loo, linen cupboard & under-stair storage

This home brings together high-end design, comfort, and everyday functionality in one of the most desirable locations in the area. Whether you’re upsizing, downsizing, or looking for your first move into a secure estate, this one is definitely worth a closer look.
