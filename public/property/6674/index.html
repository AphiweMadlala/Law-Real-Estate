About This Property

This gem ground floor apartment offers comfortable and convenient living in a well-maintained complex with panoramic views throughout in a superb setting and desirable location. Making it an excellent option for first-time buyers , investors or those looking to downsize. The unit features two well-sized bedrooms with built-in wardrobes and a neatly finished bathroom.

The open-plan kitchen flows seamlessly into the living area, creating a practical and welcoming space for everyday living. The spacious lounge opens onto a private patio with a braai area, ideal for entertaining or enjoying relaxed outdoor time at home. Being on the ground floor adds ease of access and convenience.
Residents have access to a communal swimming pool that’s nearby, perfect for warm summer days and the complex is pet-friendly, subject to approval. The property is well located and within close proximity to a variety of amenities, including shopping centres, medical facilities and main transport routes are easily accessible. Schools are also conveniently located nearby.

A low maintenance property overall, that’s perfect for those on the go. At this price point within this sought after area, we don’t expect this one to last. Get in touch today to schedule an exclusive viewing.
