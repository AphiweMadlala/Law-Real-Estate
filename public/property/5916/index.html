About This Property

This magnificent Eco property offers a blend of Boutique hotel & Country style living. Both merged and combined together to create a one of a kind masterpiece.

This significant property is set on a 2000m2 stand with over 1000m2 under roof. It has the most rare and unique architectural functionality & flair.

On entering the huge oak front doors, your breath is simply taken away. The home has a commanding entry foyer with 4 level depth ceiling height. Gourmet High end kitchen leads onto open plan dining room & magnificent TV room. These spaces all open up seamlessly onto your exceptional entertainment area with sparkling custom pool complimented by a waterfall and a hidden bar. These back seamlessly onto a fire pit & boma area which make entertaining beyond something to look forward to.

The home has 5 gorgeous and lavish all en suite bedrooms with the most incredible finishes, functionality and feel.

There is also a hidden rooftop entertainers chill patio with 360 degree views.

The home was designed to be a part of its natural ECO setting with many surprises along the way.

In todays market it is very rare that a property with such a unique and distinct design changes hands. This incredible asset is capped off by rare to find five garages and is completely off the grid.

PLUS it has a full downstairs spectacular work from home office / cottage.

This magnificent property is a must to view for the discerning purchaser.
