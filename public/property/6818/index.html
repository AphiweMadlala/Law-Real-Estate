About This Property

Modern One-Bedroom Apartment in The Cambridge, Bryanston

Whether you're looking for your first home or a smart addition to your property portfolio, this well-positioned third-floor apartment offers comfortable, low-maintenance living in one of Bryanston's most sought-after lifestyle estates.

One of only four apartments on the floor, the unit enjoys an added sense of privacy and tranquillity. Step inside to a bright, open-plan living area where the modern kitchen flows seamlessly into the lounge, creating a welcoming space for everyday living and entertaining. The lounge opens onto a covered balcony with pleasant elevated views.

The kitchen is well designed with space for three appliances and a fridge, and comes fully equipped with a dishwasher, washing machine, and tumble dryer included in the sale.

The spacious bedroom is privately positioned and features an en-suite bathroom with a shower.

Additional features include:

- 1 Covered parking bay
- Ample visitors' parking
- Pre-paid electricity

Residents of The Cambridge enjoy access to exceptional lifestyle amenities, including a swimming pool, fully equipped gym, on-site restaurant, and beautifully maintained communal spaces.

Offering secure estate living, modern convenience, and strong rental appeal, this apartment presents an excellent opportunity for both homeowners and investors alike.
