About This Property

Designed for effortless family living and premium entertaining, the home offers generous open-plan spaces, oversized balconies, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow. Natural stone finishes, warm wood cabinetry, and expansive glass openings create a sophisticated yet welcoming atmosphere throughout the home.

The standout feature is the luxurious master suite — a private retreat complete with a massive walk-in dressing room, spa-inspired bathroom, freestanding bath, double vanities, and access to an expansive balcony overlooking the surrounding greenery.

Adding to the home’s entertainment appeal are dedicated his-and-hers bar areas, creating a unique and highly functional entertaining experience ideal for hosting on both an intimate and larger scale.

Additional features include:

* 4 Spacious bedrooms
* 4 Luxury bathrooms
* Exceptional master suite with dressing room
* Dedicated his-and-hers bar areas
* Multiple entertainment terraces and balconies
* Designer bathrooms with freestanding baths
* Expansive open-plan living spaces
* Swimming pool and outdoor entertainment areas
* Multi-level architectural design with exceptional proportions
* Premium finishes throughout
* Secure parking and excellent privacy
* Positioned on a 1,348m² stand

Set within one of Constantia Kloof’s most elevated and established residential pockets, this residence offers a rare combination of luxury, scale, privacy, and lifestyle-focused living.

A statement home designed for buyers who appreciate space, entertaining, and refined living.
