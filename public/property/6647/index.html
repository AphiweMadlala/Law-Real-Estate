About This Property

This beautiful second-floor apartment is located in a secure, newly built complex offering modern finishes and excellent lifestyle amenities.

The home features an open-plan kitchen with granite countertops, a gas stove, and ample cupboard space. The spacious living area flows out onto a large covered balcony—perfect for relaxing or entertaining.

Both bedrooms are generously sized, fully tiled, and fitted with built-in cupboards. The main bedroom includes a full en-suite bathroom with both a shower and bathtub. The second bathroom is well-appointed and services the rest of the home.

The complex offers exceptional facilities including a restaurant, clubhouse, communal swimming pool, and a dedicated play area for children.

Conveniently located close to Carlswald Shopping Centre with quick and easy access to the N1 via New Road.
