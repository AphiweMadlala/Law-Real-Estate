About This Property

Step into the embrace of nature's tranquility, away from the hustle and bustle of city life and traffic noise. Embrace this entertainers delight as your own, a home where relaxation beckons.

Welcome to this delightful and opulent family abode, ideal for those who relish in hosting and cherish spaciousness and security. Nestled within a prestigious estate, this luxurious home which boasts generously sized bedrooms and the lifestyle that estate living affords.

The residence features two interconnected family rooms, seamlessly flowing into a dining area, perfect for entertaining. The entertainment space is sure to captivate even the most discerning guests, complemented by a spacious modern kitchen boasting a luxurious granite-topped island. This gorgeous kitchen is the heartbeat of the home with a lovely flow. Complete with ample counter space and designated areas for appliances

Ascend the sunlit passage to discover the private sleeping quarters. Five spacious bedrooms await, two offering modern en suite bathrooms with bath and showers.

Both the family bathroom and the expansive en-suite primary bathroom offer a comprehensive array of amenities, including large tubs, spacious showers, and double his and her bathroom basins with storage.

Upstairs is a large family room with a bar, fireplace and balcony with built in braai, overlooking the immaculately manicured garden, complete with a sparkling pool, jacuzzi and a separate Lapa area for outdoor relaxation and dining.

Access to the property is facilitated by a secure entrance, comprising two garages and three open parking bays, all automated for convenience and seamlessly integrated into the home.

This immaculate home is solar powered!

Clearview Estate provides a serene country living ambiance, with convenient proximity to amenities, schools, Silverstar Casino, Cradlestone Mall, and hospitals, all within 10km.

This meticulously managed estate offers 24-hour guarded access, ensuring peace of mind. Reach out to schedule a viewing and embark on a lifestyle like no other.
