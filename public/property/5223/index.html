About This Property

SIMPLY BREATHE TAKING!!!

This triple story family home is situated on a 3000sqm stand, with 2000sqm under roof and is located in the sought after Kolberg strip. Quite simply a Mastery of perfection with a contemporary design, encapsulated in glass to exploit the breathtaking views of the golf course from all the bedrooms and living areas

Nestled between magnificent rock outcrops and existing trees. This home is designed and anchored around a generous double volume open plan living area served on either side by kitchen and living room areas with the site dropping away in front of the house overlooking the picturesque views of the South.

Be welcomed by double volume ceilings into an expertly designed space featuring a host of premium features including suspended light fittings offering the most beautiful lighting to the home

Elegant and minimalistic, yet warm and inviting. The interior spaces offer perfect flow through full length stacker doors onto expansive balcony’s and enclosed boma area. Blurring the divide between inside and out

Entertaining is a dream in this home, featuring a bespoke cocktail bar, fire pit, pool and games room. The property also boasts 5 generously proportioned bedroom all en-suites, a pyjama lounge, a cinema and a gym.
