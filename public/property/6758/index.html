About This Property

Step into a bright open-plan living space that flows effortlessly onto your private balcony — perfect for both relaxing and entertaining. The well-designed kitchen offers space for one appliance and a fridge, ensuring practical and easy everyday living.
The spacious bedroom features contemporary built-in cupboards, while the bathroom is fitted with both a bath and shower, offering added comfort and convenience.
Residents of Greenlee Eco Estate enjoy an exceptional range of lifestyle amenities, including a communal swimming pool, fully equipped gym, clubhouse, and braai area — all within a secure estate with 24-hour access control.
This apartment is ideal for first-time buyers, young professionals, or investors seeking low-maintenance living in one of Sandton’s most accessible and well-designed developments.
