About This Property

Boasting four spacious bedrooms and three well-appointed bathrooms, the home features expansive open-plan reception areas including a welcoming family lounge, private study/office, and a sophisticated bar area complete with a wine cellar and counter – perfect for entertaining.

The heart of the home is its gourmet kitchen, which includes a central island, separate scullery, and walk-in pantry. Additional premium touches include underfloor heating, air-conditioning throughout, a cozy fireplace, and a heated fiberglass swimming pool nestled in a beautifully landscaped private garden with irrigation and a borehole.

Practical features are equally impressive: staff bathroom, four-car garage parking, and state-of-the-art utilities including solar backup power and a water filtration system.

Located in the sought-after, access-controlled Palomino Crescent enclosure in Meyersdal, this exceptional clinker brick home offers a rare combination of style, space, and top-tier security. With 24-hour guarded security, electric fencing, alarm system, CCTV, and a comprehensive filtration and backup solar system, this home is designed for peace of mind and modern living.

This property is the perfect blend of luxury, security, and functionality – ideal for the discerning family seeking comfort in one of Meyersdal’s most exclusive enclaves.
