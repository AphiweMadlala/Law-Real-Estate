About This Property

Be mesmerised as you walk through your double volume entrance into your home. The beauty of the layout is that the entire home had been designed to have the best of both worlds, open plan living following seemlessly onto the patio and gorgeous living area that combines the kitchen, dining space, and lounge.

The beautiful kitchen includes a separate scullery for extra convenience, while the lounge has a charming fireplace, ideal for fostering warm and welcoming moments. The lounge features a sliding door that leads to a large patio with a built-in braai, perfect for hosting gatherings. From this spot, enjoy the sight of the glistening heated pool and the elegantly designed garden.

Four spacious bedrooms may be found upstairs. A private balcony is accessible from two of these guestrooms, making them ideal for taking in morning coffee or evening sunsets. To accommodate your family's demands, the house also has two contemporary bathrooms.

This incredible home offers four air conditioners to ensure comfort, a double garage, and a state-of-the-art solar system.

La Como Lifestyle Estate is a neighbourhood enhanced with amenities like restaurants, a gym, and an indoor pool, making it more than just a place to live. Take advantage of the estate's close vicinity to important facilities, such as prestigious schools, major highways, and shopping centres, and enjoy leisurely walks inside it.

This outstanding property provides unparalleled luxury, safety, and a lifestyle perfect for discerning homeowners in search of peace and sophistication within a prestigious setting.
