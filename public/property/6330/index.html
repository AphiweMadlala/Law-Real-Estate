About This Property

Experience unparalleled luxury and exclusivity in this one-of-one penthouse at The Houghton—Johannesburg’s premier address for secure, high-end living. Spanning 460m² over two levels, this exceptional four-bedroom penthouse redefines modern elegance with its private rooftop extension, direct stair and lift access to both floors, and breathtaking views.

A Truly Unique Living Experience

This penthouse is unlike any other in The Houghton, boasting an enclosed rooftop extension that seamlessly integrates indoor and outdoor living. The top-floor addition includes an extra en-suite bedroom, a private entertainment space, and an expansive pool deck, offering a rare and exclusive layout within the development.

Lower-Level Sophistication

The open-plan lower level is designed for both comfort and functionality. A state-of-the-art kitchen with integrated appliances, an ergonomic layout, and a hidden scullery flows effortlessly into the dining and living areas, all opening onto a sun-drenched balcony that showcases uninterrupted views of Johannesburg’s scenic skyline, lush tree canopies, and championship golf course.

This level also features three spacious en-suite bedrooms, each thoughtfully designed with ample storage. The north-facing master suite is a true sanctuary, offering panoramic views of the rolling greens and the majestic Sandton skyline.

Exclusive Rooftop Retreat

A floating staircase (or private lift access) leads to the top-floor entertainment haven, where stacking glass doors create a seamless transition between the elegant indoor lounge and bar and the outdoor entertainment deck with a private pool. This floor is also home to the fourth en-suite bedroom, making it the only penthouse in The Houghton with this exclusive layout.
