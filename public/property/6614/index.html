About This Property

In this exclusive Higgovale setting, this masterpiece offers it all: luxury, lifestyle, and views that will leave you mesmerised. Proudly presented under Law exclusive mandate, this is a residence that redefines the Cape Town iconic lifestyle dream.

Three Levels of Distinction
Set across three magnificent levels, the home comprises four bedrooms and three-and-a-half bathrooms. Each space has been thoughtfully designed to maximise comfort, entertainment, and awe-inspiring vistas.

Lower Level: Entertainer’s Paradise
The ground floor boasts a quaint wine cellar and entertainment lounge that flows seamlessly onto an expansive outdoor deck. Here, a sparkling pool and generous entertaining area frame panoramic views, perfect for hosting or simply unwinding in style.

Middle Level: Elevated Living
The heart of the home reveals soaring ceiling heights and an abundance of open-plan spaces. A front lounge wraps around with 270 degree views that stretch across the city and Table Mountain, with a glimpse of the ocean in the distance. The dining area blends effortlessly with a high-end designer kitchen, showcasing solid wood finishes and premium fittings. An adjoining study nook flows directly off the kitchen and dining zone, making it an ideal workspace that remains connected to the life of the home. This level also features two cosy fireplaces and hosts the luxurious master suite, complete with walk-in closet, indulgent en suite, and access to the shared balcony that extends from the living area.

Upper Level: Family Comfort
Upstairs, two generously sized bedrooms enjoy sweeping views in both directions and share a well-appointed bathroom, an ideal setup for children or guests.

Additional Features
The property is complete with luxurious staff accommodation, two secure garaged bays, a large storeroom, and both front and back outdoor entertainment spaces, ensuring ultimate versatility and convenience.

The Offering in Closing
With its rare combination of scale, location, and finish, this home delivers more than one could ever expect, especially at its incredible listing price. This is more than a home; it is a lifestyle opportunity without equal.
