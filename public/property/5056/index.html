About This Property

The word that fully describes this home is Stunning!

Situated on a oversized stand in the desirable Mount Pellan drive boom overlooking the Glenvista golf course, this home is sublimely magical!

Designed with an emphasis on natural light, one immediately notices the sun, golf course and mountain framed throughout this home.

The first floor open plan design entertains elegantly, summer and winter, fully opening up to a large deck boasting a sparkling blue pool and pergola. This floor brings the fun home with your own personal night club. Sip sun-downers on the well-positioned deck and indulge your senses with the many majestic sunsets over the mountains

The second floor is simply breathe-taking and includes a state of the art kitchen, separate scullery, open plan dining room and living room, 3 bedrooms , 2 en-suits both leading out to magnificently positioned balcony’s overlooking the swimming pool and golf course

The upper level consists of a large master bedroom with an en-suite bathroom, walk in dressing area, massive private lounge and balcony with superlative views

Additional features include full Solar Power, Water Tanks, Air-conditioning throughout, full 1 bedroom cottage, staff quarters and garaging for 7 cars

A floating roof deck caps off a home which is simply one of the best homes in the hills of Glenvista
