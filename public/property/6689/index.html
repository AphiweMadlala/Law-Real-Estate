About This Property

This beautifully designed modern ground-floor apartment offers effortless luxury and everyday convenience in the sought-after area of Benoni North. Boasting two spacious bedrooms and a stylish full bathroom, the home features a sleek open-plan kitchen with contemporary finishes, flowing seamlessly into a bright living area that opens onto a private patio, ideal for relaxed outdoor living or entertaining.

Thoughtfully designed for low-maintenance living, the apartment is pet-friendly and fitted with a solar system, as well as prepaid water and electricity for added control and efficiency. Covered parking and 24-hour security provide peace of mind, making this an ideal lock-up-and-go home for first-time buyers or young professionals with busy lifestyles.

Residents enjoy exclusive access to premium communal amenities, including a sparkling swimming pool, elegant clubhouse, and a well-equipped children’s playground, creating a balanced lifestyle of comfort, leisure, and community.

Perfectly positioned, the apartment is located just minutes from OR Tambo International Airport, making it ideal for frequent travellers. Popular dining destinations, top medical facilities, Brentwood Park Shopping Centre, and a selection of reputable primary and high schools are all within close proximity, ensuring unmatched convenience for everyday living.

This exceptional home combines modern style, smart living, and an enviable location, offering a refined yet practical lifestyle in one of Benoni North’s most desirable developments.
