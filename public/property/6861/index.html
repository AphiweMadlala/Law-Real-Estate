About This Property

This ideally located unit is perfect from top to bottom. You even have your own staircase for the ultimate privacy!

The entire unit has been retiled with large porcelain tiles and freshly painted.

A beautiful open kitchen with granite tops is the show stopper.... With space for appliances and a big pantry cupboard.
Breakfast nook with seating area ideal to share family meals.

The living area is open plan and sunny leading to a lovely balcony area with a built in braai. This is a perfect spot to enjoy summer evenings with friends!

There are two spacious bedrooms with custom Cabinetry and the main bedroom even has an additional tall boy that will remain.

The bathroom is exquisite. With beautiful tiling, a large shower, basin and toilet. Hans grohe taps and incredible finishing.

There is a 1kva inverter that will remain, ideal in the event of a power outage.

The complex is beautifully maintained, with 24 hour security and loads of parking, it's a wonderful community to be part of. There is also an exceptional pool area that can be used for functions. This unit has one designated carport as well.

The complex is located across the road from the Thaba Eco Village and is a stones throw away from the new Pantry in Bassonia. Ideally located for easy commute to any major highways. Excellent schools and fabulous amenities close by.

Don't delay to come view this property it won't be on the market very long!
