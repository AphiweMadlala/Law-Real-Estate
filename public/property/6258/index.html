About This Property

This contemporary masterpiece is set on 3 levels, situated on one of Bryanston’s most sought-after roads, stands as a beacon of luxury and contemporary design.

Set within an exclusive enclave of just 10 homes, this property epitomizes sophistication and status, offering an unparalleled living experience.

Ground Floor: A Grand Entrance
As you step inside, you are welcomed by a breathtaking double-volume lounge bathed in natural light, streaming through expansive glass doors and windows. The seamless open-plan layout connects the lounge to the sleek, modern kitchen, which features top-of-the-line imported finishes, gas countertops, and Miele appliances.

Beyond the kitchen lies the formal dining area, which flows effortlessly to the outdoor entertainment space. Grand double-glazed doors lead to a wooden deck surrounding the sparkling pool and fire pit, creating a perfect setting for relaxation and entertainment. The low-maintenance, water-conscious garden adds to the charm of this serene outdoor oasis.

Adjacent to the kitchen, a spacious second lounge offers versatility and can be easily converted into a fifth bedroom, complete with its own bathroom.

Second Floor: Tranquil Living Spaces
A striking modern staircase, with a glass-enclosed wine cellar beneath it, leads to the second floor, where comfort meets elegance. This level boasts engineered wood flooring and air-conditioning throughout, ensuring a cozy and luxurious atmosphere.

To the left, a pajama lounge offers a relaxing retreat, while the opulent master suite is a sanctuary of indulgence. Featuring a marble-clad bathroom and a private balcony, the master bedroom embodies sophistication and comfort.

The floor also includes three additional chic en-suite bedrooms, all flooded with natural light. Two of these bedrooms share a balcony overlooking the pool and offering stunning views of the Johannesburg skyline. A thoughtfully placed study nook between two of the bedrooms creates a practical yet stylish workspace.

Rooftop Escape
The top level of the home unveils a spectacular rooftop entertainment area. Fitted with wooden decking and seating, it provides the perfect spot to enjoy the panoramic easterly views of Johannesburg and beyond.

This property truly represents the pinnacle of modern living, offering an exceptional blend of luxury, functionality, and design. It’s not just a home it’s a lifestyle.
