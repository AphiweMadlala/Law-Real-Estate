About This Property

Welcome to this extraordinary two-bedroom, two-bathroom ground-floor unit in the sought-after Bakara Complex a true sanctuary where modern comfort meets natural tranquility. Perfectly positioned in a quiet corner of the estate, this rare corner block unit offers unmatched peace, privacy, and a lifestyle surrounded by nature.

Boasting only one neighboring ground unit and its own beautiful private deck, this home provides a calm, exclusive retreat rarely found in sectional living.

Step inside to discover an immaculately maintained residence featuring modern finishes throughout, a stylish kitchen with Caesarstone countertops, ample cupboard space, and room for all your appliances. The breakfast nook seamlessly connects to the open-plan living and TV area, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere perfect for relaxing or entertaining.

White slider doors lead you out to your private entertainment patio and deck, where you can unwind and enjoy a serene outlook over the estate’s lush natural surrounds.

The main bedroom, positioned to capture the tranquil views, includes a luxurious en suite bathroom with a shower, basin, and toilet. The second bedroom is generously sized with built-in cupboards and is serviced by a second full bathroom featuring a bath, shower, basin, and toilet.

This is more than a home — it’s a lifestyle defined by peace, privacy, and beauty.

Spacious open-plan living area with Caesarstone kitchen
