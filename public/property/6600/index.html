About This Property

Step into a world of functional family living with this stunning four-bedroom home, designed to offer both style and functionality. Upon entrance, you're welcomed into an open plan living area with a fireplace and dining area that flows effortlessly into a beautifully appointed kitchen, complete with a convenient scullery—ideal for seamless entertaining and everyday ease.
This home boasts four generously sized bedroom and two bathrooms, guest toilet, providing ample space for family and guests alike. The luxurious main bedroom is en-suite with a study area as well as walk-in dresser.
Your outdoor living blend harmoniously in this residence, with an inviting entertainment area that spills out onto a sparkling blue salt chlorinator pool, pond and a lapa with cozy seating area for about fifty guests, it’s the perfect setting for hosting or relaxing in your own private oasis.
Additionally, the property includes a separate Zozo with electricity connection, one bedroom staff quarters with bathroom and Jojo tanks offering added convenience and flexibility. Every corner of this home has been thoughtfully crafted to balance modern design with the art of comfortable living, promising a lifestyle of sophistication and leisure.
