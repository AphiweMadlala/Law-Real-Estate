About This Property

Situated on a large stand in a sought-after part of Bedfordview, this substantial family home is perfectly positioned close to the convenience and reputable schools just off Van Buuren Road. Designed to easily accommodate a large or extended family, this residence boasts a total of six generously proportioned bedrooms and 5.5 modern bathrooms. Practicality is key, with four of the six bedrooms featuring their own private en-suite bathrooms, ensuring comfort and convenience for all family members.

Accommodation and Practical Features

The spacious main bedroom serves as a comfortable retreat, offering a practical walk-in closet and a modern full bathroom with a relaxing Jacuzzi bath.

Key Features for Family Living:

• Dual Kitchens: A highly practical feature for multi-generational living, large-scale entertaining, or catering needs.

• Flexible Living Spaces: The home includes a large Living Room, a separate formal Tea Lounge, and a dedicated Study/Office, providing versatile areas for different family activities and working from home.

Outdoor Enjoyment and Security

The property delivers a superb outdoor lifestyle with a sparkling swimming pool and a substantial large garden - a secure and beautiful space for children, pets, and outdoor gatherings.

Practicalities are well-covered with secure access via security gates, a four-car garage providing ample, secure parking, and a maid's quarters (staff accommodation).

This is a well-located property that delivers massive size and potential, ready to become the long-term family base you’ve been searching for in Bedfordview.
