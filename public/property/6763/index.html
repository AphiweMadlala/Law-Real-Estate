About This Property

Set in what is arguably one of the finest positions in Meyersdal Eco Estate, this exceptional home is perfectly located in a quiet cul-de-sac with only one neighbour, offering uninterrupted greenbelt frontage and breathtaking sunset views. The expansive, level landscaped garden is truly world-class a rare find that elevates this home into a league of its own.

Designed for luxury, space, and effortless entertaining, the property offers two separate entrances and six garages, including a glass show garage — ideal for the discerning car enthusiast.

A striking glass cylindrical entrance with a feature staircase sets the tone on arrival, while natural light floods the home, enhancing the sense of volume and depth throughout. The open-plan kitchen and dining area form the heart of the home, seamlessly connecting to multiple large entertainment spaces and private cinema room.

These include a spacious games/billiards room opening onto the garden, a beautiful TV lounge overlooking the pool and landscaped grounds, and an expansive covered patio designed to maximise both space and views. A stylish bar further enhances the entertainment offering.

The home boasts a variety of living areas, including a formal lounge, dining room, family lounge, study, and private office, complemented by four fireplaces throughout.

Upstairs accommodation comprises 4 generously sized bedrooms, all en-suite, with brand new carpets installed. All with the most magnificent balconies drawing in the natural setting and view from every angle.

Additional features include:
&bull; Fully self-contained flatlet/cottage with private entrance (bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, lounge)
&bull; Staff accommodation with shower
&bull; Air-conditioning, underfloor heating & fireplaces
&bull; 30 solar panels, 30kW inverter & 20kW battery backup

This is a rare opportunity to secure a home with unmatched position, views, and lifestyle offering.
