About This Property

This expansive 21,451 sqm property on ERF 3578, Clayville Ext 33, offers an ideal site for a residential development. Positioned along Bismuth Street with frontage on Niobium Street and Zinc Place, the land is zoned Residential 3, with approvals in place for up to 180 units across three stories—suited for one, two, and three-bedroom apartments.

Key Property Highlights:

- Zoning & Approvals: Zoned Residential 3 and approved for a 180-unit, three-story configuration, with all building plans included.

- Infrastructure Ready: Geotechnical reports, bulk services, township establishment compliance, and environmental assessments (including WULA) are complete, meaning the site is fully prepared for development.

- Prime Location in Clayville: Known for its strategic mix of residential and light commercial properties, Clayville combines convenience with affordability. Situated close to major roads, schools, shopping, and industrial hubs, it offers a great balance for residents seeking accessibility to Johannesburg, Pretoria, and surrounding areas.

Included Documentation:

This sale comes with a complete set of development documents, covering all necessary requirements:

- Zoning & Beacon Certificates, General Plan
- Project Program, Professional Team Appointments, and BOQ
- Municipal and Engineering Submissions
- Compliance with Township Establishment
- Coordinated Site Development Plan (SDP)
- Full Building Plans
- Finishes Schedule and Structural Soundness Reports
- Geotechnical, Environmental, and Topography Reports
- Professional Indemnity Cover, GLS, and Funding Disclaimers
- Project Organogram and Engineering Works

Investment Potential: For developers looking to capture the demand for affordable housing in a growing, accessible area, this property is development-ready and positioned to deliver strong returns in one of Midrand’s rising neighbourhood’s.
