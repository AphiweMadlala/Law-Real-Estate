About This Property

The Perfect Blend of Style & Practicality.

Offering stylish, low-maintenance living in a secure complex, this beautifully presented townhouse combines modern finishes with a number of thoughtful upgrades that make everyday living both comfortable and convenient.

The downstairs living area is bright and spacious, with high ceilings, quality Traviata RCB XL Traviloc flooring and an easy open-plan layout. Large glass doors create a wonderful connection to the private patio and splash pool, making the outdoor space an ideal spot to unwind or entertain.

The modern kitchen is well appointed with ample cupboard and worktop space, a breakfast counter and a separate scullery/laundry area. A Siemens iQ700 built-in oven with steam and microwave functions adds an excellent touch of practicality.

Upstairs are two generously sized bedrooms, both en-suite, with the main bedroom enjoying a private balcony and ample built-in cupboards. Luxaflex Duette blinds are fitted throughout the home, with blackout blinds in both bedrooms, providing excellent privacy and light control.

The home is particularly well equipped for energy and water independence, featuring 12 solar panels with a 6.5kW solar system, a 4.6kW hybrid inverter and 21kWh of battery storage. Two 750-litre water tanks, a variable-speed pump and whole-house water filtration with backup plumbing further enhance the property's self-sufficiency.

A double automated garage with upgraded modular flooring completes the property. A practical layout that makes it equally suited to a young family, professional couple or those looking to downsize without compromising on space.
