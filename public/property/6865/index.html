About This Property

A welcoming courtyard alongside a double garage takes you into this awesome home, combining comfort with convenience.

The large well-appointed separate kitchen, with room for a pantry, opens to the living area : a modern tiled dining area and relaxing lounge with a guest toilet and niche corner – bar area / reader’s corner / small office? This leads to an inviting entertainer’s patio and private garden which takes you to the fenced off sparkling pool.

The 3 upstairs bedrooms are spacious with ample cupboard space and large windows allowing natural sunlight to brighten these restful spaces. There are 2 well-appointed bathrooms : the en-suite one has a bath, the shared one is a full bathroom. There is also a large room which can be used as an executive home office or 4th bedroom

Neat, modern and move-in ready, the home also has an invertor, a water tank, a lift, an outside storeroom, and secure visitors parking. Wide pavements and green spaces abound, and the nearby parks provide relaxation and quiet reflection. The incredible view of the setting sun behind the Sandton skyline invites you to enjoy all that the Sandton night life has to offer

This awesome home is in a secure quiet cul-de-sac in a most central location on the border of Sandton and Johannesburg, perfectly positioned just minutes from various business hubs, shopping centers , restaurants, entertainment/gym facilities, medical facilities, schools, and places of worship. Being central to all highways and the Gautrain means easy access to wider area.
