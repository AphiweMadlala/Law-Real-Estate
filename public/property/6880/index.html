About This Property

Beautifully positioned within this desirable complex and set amongst manicured gardens, with surrounding panoramic views from the apartment and throughout this well-maintained complex that offers peace of mind with 24/7 access controlled security.

Upon entry into this natural light filled home, you are welcomed into expansive open plan living areas that flow seamlessly from a well laid out kitchen consisting of ample work space and storage space, as well as a breakfast nook. Spacious dining and lounge areas open out onto a covered patio and a communal garden.
