About This Property

Set on a quiet, tree-lined street in one of Parkwood’s most desirable pockets, this exceptional family residence effortlessly blends timeless charm with modern design. From the moment you arrive, the home offers a sense of warmth, space and privacy that’s becoming increasingly rare.

Beautiful natural light fills the interiors, where soaring ceilings, exposed trusses, original wooden floors and elegant proportions create an inviting atmosphere throughout. The heart of the home is a beautifully renovated kitchen, thoughtfully designed with generous workspace, quality finishes and a large central island that naturally becomes the gathering place for family and friends.

Offering four generous bedrooms and three well-appointed bathrooms, the home has been designed for comfortable family living, while the separate cottage provides endless flexibility as a guest suite, home office, studio or income-generating opportunity.

Outside, established gardens and expansive lawns provide a peaceful setting for children to play, entertaining on warm summer afternoons or simply enjoying the tranquillity that makes Parkwood so sought after.

Perfectly positioned just moments from Kingsmead College, St Teresa’s School and St John’s College, with Rosebank’s caf&eacute;s, restaurants, shopping and business district only minutes away, this is a home that offers both exceptional convenience and an enviable lifestyle.

Homes of this calibre in such a tightly held location seldom become available.
