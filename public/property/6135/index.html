About This Property

We are proud to present this remarkable family residence located on the prestigious Eccleston Crescent, one of Bryanston’s most sought-after addresses. Set within an exclusive estate of only six homes, this property enjoys one of the finest positions in Bryanston, offering both privacy and serenity.

Designed with convenience and effortless living in mind, this single-level masterpiece spans 500 square meters under roof and is set on a generous 1,192 square meter stand. The home’s architectural design is truly exceptional, featuring a striking U-shaped layout that enhances the flow of the living spaces.

As you enter, you are greeted by exposed beams and an elegant entryway that leads to a formal lounge on the left and a dining room on the right, creating an inviting atmosphere for both family life and entertaining guests. The home boasts four spacious, luxurious en-suite bedrooms, each finished to the highest standard.

The heart of this home is its stunning outdoor entertainment area. Step through the front door and you’ll be drawn to the magnificent patio, complete with a sparkling pool and beautifully landscaped garden. This outdoor space is the perfect extension of the indoor living areas, seamlessly connecting the entire home while offering a tranquil, private retreat for relaxation and gatherings.

The main bedroom is a true sanctuary, featuring a large walk-in closet and a sumptuous en-suite bathroom designed for ultimate comfort. The kitchen, designed for both functionality and style, is ideal for family gatherings and entertaining, and it opens up to a spacious TV room with a Morso wood-burning fireplace—perfect for creating a warm ambiance during chilly winter evenings. Your internal living space is topped off with a basement which can be used as a cinema, playroom or man cave.

Additional features of this property include large interleading garages, a backup power generator, and full staff quarters, ensuring both convenience and peace of mind. Pool has solar heating and invertor. Security features full camera system for on- and off-site monitoring.

This home is a rare opportunity to own a meticulously designed residence in one of Bryanston’s most exclusive settings. Don’t miss out on the chance to make this extraordinary property your own.
