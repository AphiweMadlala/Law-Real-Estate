About This Property

Nestled on Linksfield Ridge, with views all the way to Sandton city and Pretoria and a rich deep history and tales of Mahatma Gandhi staying on this very property, this is truly a unique property. The property was redeveloped by a family only a short 12 yrs ago however most of the family have since left SA. The property has maintained its heritage “old world feel” on the outside whilst embracing the new ultra-modern living one would expect from a home of this stature on the inside. The property in total has approximately 1600m under roof including garage spaces (4 garage spaces).
The centrepiece of this estate is an exquisite main house, featuring spacious living areas, opulent finishes, and breathtaking views that create an atmosphere of serenity and prestige.
Complementing the main residence are two beautifully appointed smaller homes on the property.

The property consists of 4 main structures and 2 smaller structures.

Building 1 Main House -
4 rooms all en-suite. The main bedroom has a jacuzzi linked to the main bedroom.
The main house has copper pipes in the flooring of the house which heat the home during winter. Also a walk in fridge, x2 micro waves and an entertainers stove. The home has been designed to entertain in so apart from a 12 seater dining table all the rooms open onto each other and open onto the patio to make for an entertainers dream home.
The home is backed up by a full generator, solar and Lithium batteries and a borehole.

Building 2 - Consists currently of -
Downstairs, boardroom to seat 12, executive office, secretary office and small meeting room and kitchen.
Upstairs, Gym and toilet.
Depending on requirements this area could easily be turned into a second home.
Building 3 - has 2 maids rooms. 1 well sized and well fitted and 1 smaller. With 3 downstairs garages.
Building 4 - is a guest cottage that has a bedroom en-suite with a kitchenette and a living room with a pull out couch as well as a dining area. It also houses a garage downstairs.
The 1st smaller structure sits across the pool area and is a “light” entertainment area for the kids. It has a small snooker table and a sound system.
The 2nd structure is the heated 25m rim flow pool with a wine cellar below.
Security -
- a fully equipped guard house at the gate with windows protruding into the street to allow the guard full visibility of the comings and goings.
- A State of art alarm system linked to an area specific security company.
- An area specific pro active dedicated patrol vehicle at night. Zero crime at the property ever.
- ⁠a perimeter camera system that is monitored after hours by a remote site monitoring centre that has communication to the area reaction company.
- ⁠the area currently has an application in to become a “gated community” which should take another 4-6 months to approval and will have 24hr manned security at the entrance to the suburb. This should greatly enhance the value of the property.
Located in a boomed-off area with 24/7 security patrols, this estate offers peace of mind for its residents. Additional security measures ensure privacy and protection at all times.
The home also has some beautiful mountain paths to stroll around and is a gardeners dream.
The property is equipped with solar power and a generator, ensuring uninterrupted energy supply while maintaining eco-conscious living. A borehole provides independent water access.

The property is within
- 5mins of Eastgate Shopping mall (Regional mall)
- ⁠10mins (no traffic) of O R Tambo airport
- ⁠3 mins Linksfield Hospital (Netcare) and many more hospitals in close proximity.
- ⁠close proximity to many private and prestigious schools, like St John and St Mary’s and Saheti.
- ⁠has good access to a number of eating establishments like The Neighbourhood 8mins.
The home has fibre wifi and gets speeds of 100mb/s up and down.
