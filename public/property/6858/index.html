About This Property

Welcome to Pebble Falls Lifestyle Estate, where comfortable modern living meets security, convenience and a relaxed lifestyle.

This well-positioned first-floor apartment offers a practical layout with plenty of natural flow between the living spaces. The open-plan kitchen connects seamlessly with the living area, creating a welcoming space for everyday living and entertaining.

Step outside onto your own private balcony, the perfect spot to enjoy your morning coffee, relax after a busy day or simply spend some time outdoors.

The apartment features two well-sized bedrooms and two bathrooms, with one bedroom offering the added convenience of an en-suite bathroom.

The estate offers a range of features designed for comfortable family living. Children can enjoy the dedicated playground, while the communal braai area provides a great space to relax and spend time with family and friends.

Security is well taken care of with 24-hour security and a boomed-off estate, giving you added peace of mind.

Parking is also well catered for, with one covered parking bay allocated to the apartment and ample visitors’ parking available for your guests.

Whether you are a first-time buyer, young professional, couple or investor, this apartment offers a great balance of comfort, convenience and lifestyle.

A home with the space you need, the security you want and the lifestyle you can enjoy.
