About This Property

This expansive estate spans an impressive 8.9 hectares of land. This extraordinary property boasts a charming 3-bedroom main house, surrounded by an ensemble of 10 delightful cottages nestled throughout the plot.

The main house features a thoughtfully designed layout, offering a comfortable and spacious living environment. With three generously sized bedrooms, residents can enjoy ample space for relaxation and privacy. The property's charming cottages add to the allure, providing a unique experience for guests or potential tenants.

Investors and developers will find this property to be an excellent opportunity to capitalize on its potential. The 10 cottages currently generate a steady monthly revenue of around R50,000/month, presenting an attractive return on investment. The income generated by these cottages provides a promising foundation for future development or expansion possibilities.

Situated alongside the enchanting Eye of Africa on two sides, this property offers unparalleled natural beauty and a serene environment. The surrounding landscapes provide a tranquil retreat for residents and visitors alike. Investors and developers will appreciate the location's potential for creating an exclusive haven, capitalizing on the captivating surroundings.

Whether you envision this property as a private sanctuary or a remarkable investment opportunity, its prime location, vast land size, and existing revenue stream make it an exceptional choice. Don't miss out on this extraordinary chance to own a piece of this remarkable landscape, ripe with potential and waiting to be transformed into something truly special.
