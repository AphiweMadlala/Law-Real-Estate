About This Property

Situated in the heart of Sandton & nestled on the 29th floor of the 4th highest building in Africa this 2 bedroom & 2.5 bathroom (all en suite) apartment is the epitome of opulence.

Upon entering, you are greeted by a spacious and open living area which is adorned with high-end finishes & decor.

Large glass sliding doors which open onto your private balcony, not only provide breath taking views of Sandton's skyline, but allows natural light to flood the space throughout the day.
The kitchen easily merges into the living area, boasting top-of-the-line appliances, moder cabinetry and premium countertops. It includes a built-in oven, stove, microwave, dishwasher, and refrigerator, all catering to the needs of any chef.

Walk upstairs and you are met by the master bedroom to the right and the secondary to the left. Both rooms with their large glass windows have taken full advantage of the impressive views from the 29th floor & the city bellow. As we enter the Second bedroom its plush carpeted floors and high end finishes are a standard throughout, including the bathroom which is modern in every aspect. Entering the master suite we are too met with the lavish carpet & en suite bathroom, which is complimented by the sunlit room & contemporary d&eacute;cor.

The building amenities include, fitness & spa centre, swimming pool, Concierge service & unique dining experience at two on site award winning restaurants.

In essence, owning an apartment in The Leonardo signifies not just a home, but a true symbol of status.
