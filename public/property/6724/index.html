About This Property

Set against the rolling hills of Glenvista, this timeless family home offers space, warmth, and exceptional potential in one of the South’s most sought-after neighbourhoods.

Designed for comfortable family living, the home features 3 to 4 well-sized bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, including a main en-suite. The versatile fourth bedroom can easily serve as a study, playroom, or work-from-home space.

The partially renovated kitchen is already underway and simply awaits your finishing touches — a fantastic opportunity to add your own style and value. Flowing from the kitchen, the spacious lounge with a fireplace and adjoining dining room create a welcoming setting.

Entertainment is effortless here. A dedicated bar area opens out to a generous garden complete with a sparkling pool and lapa, offering the perfect backdrop for weekend braais and celebrations.

Additional features include:

Double garage

Carport

Store room

Separate open-plan flatlet with its own toilet and separate shower — ideal for extended family, guests, or rental income potential

Nestled in a peaceful, elevated position, this Glenvista gem combines classic charm with exciting opportunity — ready for a family to make it their own.
