About This Property

Situated on one of the most prestigious and highly desirable roads within Cornwall Hill Country Estate, this extraordinary French Provincial style trophy residence is a masterpiece of design and scale. Set on roughly 1 hectare, the largest stand size within the estate, this home maximizes its elevated position to give 360-degree panoramic views of the surrounding area.

This architectural showpiece offers 6 expansive bedrooms and 6 bathrooms, each crafted with the highest quality finishes and meticulous attention to detail.

Designed for both grand entertaining and refined everyday living, the residence features generous open-plan living areas that flow out onto sprawling landscaped gardens and private atriums for more intimate al-fresco entertaining.

The main entertainment area of the home is situated within the Orangerie. The climate-controlled lap-pool is flanked on either side by vast entrainment areas, offering a built-in braai, bar and dining area on the one side and a bar, billiards and games room on the other. There is a home-gym, with steam-

room, sauna and a his & her bath-and-dressing-room.

Additional highlights include a four-car garage, dedicated guest parking, tennis court, two-bedroomed staff quarters, walk-in cold-room.

Combining architectural ambition, world-class finishes, this rare trophy home is a once in a lifetime acquisition.
