About This Property

A master-built home with extraordinary features culminate in perfect curves, creating feature internal spaces of distinction and flexibility.

Meticulously maintained by the current owner, inviting buyers to view and negotiate.

Fully secure grounds feature exquisite landscaping, including soothing greenery and a resort style sun terrace with swimming pool for sublime relaxation.

The materiality is top grade, and the tone is gentle for harmonious living, including state-of-the-art full home automation, allowing complete access and control if you are not home.

The lower living areas, include expanded catering capacity from its open layout kitchen (with scullery) and al-fresco areas create the ideal environment for families and guests.

This is an exceptional home where visionary design outcomes elevate the prestige of the street, which offers the family a first-class, modern lifestyle wrapped in leafy surrounds, luxury and Fort Knox security while also within close proximity to some of the suburb's best schools.

Arguably the best position within the estate, quiet, concealed and composed of only 6 luxury home’s that rarely come onto market.

Each of the generously sized bedrooms has been thoughtfully designed with ultra comfort in mind, ample closet space and large windows that welcome the outside in.

All en-suite bathrooms are complete with modern fixtures and spa-like facilities, perfect for unwinding after a long day.

Orientated to enhance the northerly aspect, natural light streams inward at every opportunity highlighting the quality and clever craftmanship, focused on maintaining the utmost privacy.

Triple garage, with extensive storage space.

Complete with double, convenient & wonderful helper accommodation.
