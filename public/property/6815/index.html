About This Property

Offering incredible proportions, endless potential and a coveted address in one of Johannesburg's most desirable suburbs, this expansive double-story residence presents a rare chance to renovate, modernize or simply enjoy one of the suburb's last remaining original homes.

A welcoming entrance hall offers a beautiful outlook onto a tranquil internal atrium, setting the tone for the generous proportions found throughout the home.

Designed with both family living and entertaining in mind, the home offers an impressive selection of reception areas, including open-plan living and dining spaces, a separate TV lounge, a formal lounge, and a billiards room complete with a built-in bar and a separate wine cellar.

The spacious kitchen features a separate scullery and an informal dining area, with direct access to a private downstairs suite comprising its own lounge, bedroom and bathroom - ideal for extended family, guests, independent teenagers or a work-from-home setup.

Upstairs, four well-proportioned bedrooms provide comfortable family accommodation. Three bedrooms share a full bathroom, while the spacious main suite enjoys a walk-in dressing room, en-suite bathroom, dedicated study, and two private balconies.

The outdoor space is where this property truly shines. Here you'll find a large covered patio overlooking the expansive garden and tennis court, creating an exceptional setting for entertaining or relaxed family living.

Additional features include a double garage with tandem parking for up to four vehicles, a workshop, and double staff accommodation.

While the home would benefit from cosmetic updating and modernization, opportunities like this are becoming increasingly scarce. Whether you're looking to restore its timeless character or create a spectacular contemporary masterpiece, this is an investment in one of Johannesburg's most desirable suburbs where location, land and long-term value are everything.
