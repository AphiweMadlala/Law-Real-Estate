About This Property

This beautiful 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom ground floor apartment offers comfortable and modern living in the heart of Sundowner, situated in a boomed-off and secure neighbourhood. The main bedroom is generously sized and features a walk-in closet, while the second bedroom is neatly tiled and ideal for a guest room or home office. The bathroom includes both a bath and a shower.
The spacious open-plan living area combines the lounge and dining space, offering a welcoming flow. The kitchen features an open layout with a breakfast nook, a pantry, washing machine space and space for a single-door fridge. The apartment also boasts a private covered patio. The undercover parking space is ideal, with plenty of visitors parking. The interior has a welcoming design.
Set in a well-maintained, cat-friendly complex, residents enjoy access to a sparkling community swimming pool and landscaped gardens. The location is exceptionally convenient—close to major roads, the Sundowner restaurant, petrol station, and within walking distance of Curro Aurora School. Whether you're a first-time buyer, downsizing, or looking for an investment, this apartment offers excellent value and a secure, relaxed lifestyle.
