About This Property

Set in the sought-after suburb of Bedfordview, this impressive three-level residence offers an exceptional combination of luxury, space, versatility and energy independence.

With four spacious bedrooms, all boasting full en-suite bathrooms, this home provides privacy and comfort for the entire family. One bedroom has its own private entrance and kitchen, making it ideal as a self-contained flatlet, guest suite or accommodation for extended family.

A Home Designed for Luxury Living

The generous living spaces include a stylish kitchen, dining room and lounge, complemented by a dedicated bar and entertainment area. A private home office provides the perfect work-from-home environment.

The home also features a house lift servicing all three levels, beautiful skylights that fill the interiors with natural light, and abundant storage space.

The Bedfordview Lifestyle

Bedfordview is renowned for its central location, established residential lifestyle and excellent access to major business, shopping and recreational destinations. Residents enjoy convenient access to leading shopping centres, restaurants, schools, medical facilities and major routes including the N3, N12 and R24, making Sandton, Johannesburg, OR Tambo International Airport and surrounding areas easily accessible.
