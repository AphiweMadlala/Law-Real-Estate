About This Property

Situated on comaro road close to the new Omni Centre, in a well maintained complex in Glenvista, this beautifully presented 152m² duplex offers the perfect combination of style, comfort, and practicality.

Boasting 3 spacious bedrooms and 2 modern bathrooms, this move-in-ready home is designed to accommodate a variety of lifestyles.

The private upstairs main bedroom is a true retreat, featuring an en-suite bathroom, a private balcony where you can unwind while taking in breathtaking sunsets or enjoy your morning coffee it offers an extended area that can easily be transformed into a cosy pyjama lounge, home office, nursery or additional living space.

Downstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms serviced by a modern family bathroom.
At the heart of the home is a light-filled open-plan kitchen, dining, and lounge area creating a warm and inviting space for everyday living and effortless entertaining.
Sliding doors open onto a covered patio overlooking one of the biggest private gardens in the complex which is perfect for relaxing, hosting family and friends or providing a secure outdoor space for children and pets.

Additional features include:
&bull;Two covered parking bays.
&bull;Excellent security within a prestigious, well-maintained complex.
&bull;A communal clubhouse and swimming pool for residents to enjoy.

Whether you're a professional working from home, simply looking for a low-maintenance lifestyle or perhaps looking to invest in one of Glenvista's most desirable complexes.

This exceptional home is ready for its new owners to move in and enjoy from day one.
